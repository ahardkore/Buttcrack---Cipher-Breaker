How it is broken

The strongest fractionation in this collection, and the hardest to attack from nothing: the 27-cell cube and the period have to be recovered together.

Frequently asked questions

How is the Trifid broken?

The strongest fractionation in this collection, and the hardest to attack from nothing: the 27-cell cube and the period have to be recovered together.

What key does the Trifid use?

keyword + period. The keyspace is unbounded in practice.

How much ciphertext do I need?

At least 90 characters for this solver to attempt it; short messages can be readable and still not be proof.
