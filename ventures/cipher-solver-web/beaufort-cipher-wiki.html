How it works

C = K - P rather than P + K, which makes the cipher its own inverse: encrypting a ciphertext with the same key returns the plaintext.

C = K - P. Reciprocal: encryption and decryption are the same operation.

A worked example

Plaintext:

MEET ME BY THE OLD CLOCK TOWER AT DAWN

Key: LEMON

Ciphertext:

ZAIV BH DO VGH QBL LAQKE UXIIX NS BMSA

This example is generated by running the cipher when the page is built, and the round trip is checked, so it always matches what the solver does.

How it is broken

Identical to Vigenere — period, then columns — with the sign of the column solve flipped.

History and context

Named for Sir Francis Beaufort, the admiral behind the wind scale, and sold as a slide rule for the Royal Navy after his death.
