Limits & verification boundaries

The browser tries selected classical methods, English statistical scoring and encoding layers. It also recognizes PK1–PK10 by exact normalized corpus equality. It cannot break properly implemented AES/RSA or prove an intended plaintext by a high score. Many wiki methods are only reference demonstrations here; their key-recovery attacks require the full version.

A supplied-key reading is arithmetic under your assumption, not independent confirmation. A worked-example reference is labelled as a build-time lookup, never a newly recovered answer. Rankings show actual returned candidates from a bounded search, which may contain fewer than five.

No analysis upload or account. Nothing is saved unless you choose Save below. This hosted page includes third-party advertising; use a locally served/offline copy for sensitive work.

Solver interface improvements

  • Mobile-first input and solve action, with compact navigation.
  • Simple by default; settings, candidate rankings, statistics and manual controls expand on demand.
  • Every cipher article has a Try this cipher action with its real method and full worked example.
  • Optional named investigations let you save, compare and restore attempts on your own device.
  • Explicit live-operation/reference labels, snapshot boundaries and no automatic text saving.
Optional local investigations — save, compare & restore

Explicit device-only storage, no account or cloud sync. Saved snapshots contain ciphertext, plaintext and keys; browser storage is not encrypted. Deleting browser data removes them. Restore does not run a new search or independently verify an old result.

No automatic text or result saving.

What is inside

Each volume opens with a page on technique — how to attack one-letter words, why THE is the most valuable guess you can make, which doubled letters to expect — then works from puzzles with several letters given up to puzzles with nothing at all. Every fifth puzzle is a Caesar rotation for a change of pace.

They are typeset for US Letter with answer blanks under every character, so they print cleanly and are comfortable to work on directly.
