# Working bibliography and source register

This register is a production draft. Before publication, each entry must be
checked against the edition actually consulted, with page numbers and access
dates added where applicable.

## Cryptography and cryptanalysis

- al-Kindi. *Manuscript on Deciphering Cryptographic Messages*. Ninth-century
  Arabic treatise; consult a scholarly translation and critical edition.
- Alberti, Leon Battista. *De componendis cifris*. Fifteenth-century work on
  cipher disks and changing alphabets; cite the translation used.
- Kasiski, Friedrich W. *Die Geheimschriften und die Dechiffrir-Kunst*.
  1863. Cite the edition and translated passages used.
- Kerckhoffs, Auguste. “La Cryptographie Militaire.” *Journal des Sciences
  Militaires*, 1883.
- Friedman, William F. *The Index of Coincidence and Its Applications in
  Cryptography*. 1922.
- Shannon, Claude E. “Communication Theory of Secrecy Systems.” *Bell System
  Technical Journal* 28 (1949): 656–715.
- Kahn, David. *The Codebreakers*. Revised edition. Scribner, 1996.
- Singh, Simon. *The Code Book*. Fourth Estate, 1999.

## Wartime and intelligence history

- Hinsley, F. H., and Alan Stripp, eds. *Codebreakers: The Inside Story of
  Bletchley Park*. Oxford University Press, 1993.
- Rejewski, Marian. “How Mathematicians Broke Enigma.” Translated memoir,
  consulted edition to be specified in final copy.
- National Security Agency. Public historical materials on American
  cryptologic history; cite individual pages and access dates.
- Central Intelligence Agency. Public records and institutional history used
  for provenance and context only; no classified inference is made.

## Kryptos and art context

- Sanborn, Jim. Public statements and interviews concerning *Kryptos*;
  individual publication details to be supplied for each quotation.
- CIA public documentation concerning the installation and New Headquarters
  Building; cite the exact record or page used.
- Contemporary newspaper and magazine coverage of the sculpture and its
  solutions; preserve publication date, author, and archive URL.

## Repository evidence

- Buttcrack—Cipher Breaker repository. Verification scripts, ciphertext data,
  attack logs, manuscript source, and generated figures. Repository revision
  and access date must be frozen at publication.

## Citation policy

A source is not cited merely because it is famous. Each note should state what
claim it supports and what it does not establish. Search results, anonymous
posts, generated prose, and unsourced repeated claims are not substitutes for
primary evidence.
