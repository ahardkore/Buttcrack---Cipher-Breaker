# Final editorial structure

The manuscript is organized into three reading books plus reference matter.

## Book One — The object and its history

- History and setting
- Cipher history and technical foundations
- The original Kryptos passages
- CIA, Germany, and institutional context

## Book Two — Learning to read a cipher

- Paradigm Kryptos through PK8
- Plain-language cryptanalysis guide
- Pencil-and-paper laboratory
- Methods, verification, and evidence

## Book Three — The research record

- Historical narrative and source criticism
- Images, publication, and revision
- Open investigations: PK9 and PK10

## Reference matter

- Glossary
- Bibliography
- Generated index
- Figures and tables
- Source register
- Image-rights ledger
- Reproducibility commands
- Archived reports

## Editorial rule

The polished narrative comes first. Raw repository reports are preserved as
appendices with status labels. They must not interrupt the introductory story,
and historical filenames do not override current evidence classifications.
