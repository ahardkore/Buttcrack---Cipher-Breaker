 VERDICT: no keyed-alphabet Quagmire yields English.

 Quagmire III is refuted outright -- a proof over all 26! alphabets at
 every period. That is the strongest form of result available here, and
 it removes the sculpture's own established cipher, the one K1 and K2
 use, from contention for K4.

 Quagmire I, II and IV are eliminated empirically over the alphabet
 pool -- including every keyword the sculpture nominates and every
 alphabet derivable from Sanborn's deliberate misspellings -- at every
 period 1..26, with 14 of those periods also refuted by proof.

 Scope, stated precisely: the empirical half is a dictionary sweep, so
 it rules out word-keyed alphabets, not arbitrary permutations. The
 long-period band is genuinely decided rather than skipped -- free
 residues were masked out, and scoring excluded every quadgram touching
 an anchor, so the verdict rests only on letters the cipher predicted
 and never on crib-copying.
