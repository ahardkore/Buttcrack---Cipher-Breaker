KRYPTOS K4 — APERIODIC AND PROGRESSIVE KEYSTREAMS

==============================================================================
 1. RUNNING KEY — is the implied key language at all?
==============================================================================

  The cribs hand us the key directly at 24 positions. A running key
  drawn from text must therefore look like text. English quadgram mean
  for real prose is about -2.4; uniform noise is about -5.2.

  standard  vigenere  frag@22-34 BLZCDCYYGCKAZ   -9.24
                      frag@64-74 MUYKLGKORNA     -8.11
  standard  beaufort  frag@22-34 JLJODEGKUKKKL   -8.64
                      frag@64-74 OCGGBGOKTRU     -7.52
  KRYPTOS   vigenere  frag@22-34 RDUMRIYWOYNKY   -8.35
                      frag@64-74 ELYOIECBAQK     -7.61
  KRYPTOS   beaufort  frag@22-34 WXAKGZTOAXAFD   -8.17
                      frag@64-74 RGTGNWRJLFK     -8.83

  -> Every fragment scores at the noise floor. No running key drawn from
     natural language can produce these shifts. Family eliminated.

 2. DIGIT-LIMITED KEYSTREAMS (Gromark / Gronsfeld / Nihilist)
==============================================================================

  These add decimal digits, so every shift must be 0..9.
  One crib above 9 kills the family under that alphabet.

  standard  vigenere  max shift 25  15/24 cribs exceed 9  dead
  standard  beaufort  max shift 20  14/24 cribs exceed 9  dead
  KRYPTOS   vigenere  max shift 23  11/24 cribs exceed 9  dead
  KRYPTOS   beaufort  max shift 25  14/24 cribs exceed 9  dead

  -> Eliminated under every alphabet and convention.
